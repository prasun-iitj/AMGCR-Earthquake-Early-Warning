# AMGCR Earthquake Research - Glossary

## Purpose

This glossary defines important terms used throughout the AMGCR Earthquake
Research project. It serves as a quick reference for beginners and contributors.

---

## Earthquake

A sudden release of energy within the Earth's crust that generates seismic waves.

---

## Seismology

The scientific study of earthquakes and the propagation of seismic waves through
the Earth.

---

## Seismic Wave

Energy that travels through the Earth due to an earthquake or similar event.

### P-Wave
The fastest seismic wave and the first to arrive at a seismic station.

### S-Wave
A slower seismic wave that arrives after the P-wave and often causes stronger
ground shaking.

---

## Seismic Station

A monitoring station equipped with instruments that record ground motion.

---

## Waveform

A graphical representation of ground motion recorded over time by a seismic
station.

---

## FDSN

The International Federation of Digital Seismograph Networks (FDSN) provides
standardized services for accessing earthquake catalogues and waveform data.

---

## ObsPy

An open-source Python library for processing and analysing seismological data.

---

## QuakeML

An XML-based standard format for storing earthquake event information.

---

## MiniSEED

A standard file format used to store seismic waveform recordings.

---

## StationXML

A standard XML format used to store seismic station metadata.

---

## Feature Extraction

The process of deriving meaningful numerical information from waveform data for
analysis or machine learning.

---

## EEWS

Earthquake Early Warning System — a system designed to detect earthquakes
quickly and provide advance warning before strong shaking reaches populated
areas.

---

## Reproducibility

The ability to repeat an experiment using the same data, methods, and software
to obtain comparable results.
